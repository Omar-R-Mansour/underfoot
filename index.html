How to play Underfoot

You're a little yellow flower in a crack in the pavement, and you travel by getting stepped on.

When a shoe lands on you and lifts off, your stem springs back and flings a seed. If the seed lands in a crack, it blooms into a new flower, and that flower becomes you (it wears a white ring). Every flower plays a note and the footsteps are the drums, so the street turns into a song as you cross it.

Your goal on every street: reach the curb on the far right before the clock runs out.

Controls

You only choose the direction; the shoe decides how far you fly. Timing never matters: the seed flies by itself when the shoe lifts.

Reading the street

The shoe that steps on you decides how far you fly (one paver = one square of pavement):

What steps on youHow far you fly
Sneaker1 paver
Heel2 pavers
Boot3 pavers
A crowd standing on you, waiting at a red lightThe shoe's distance + 1 for every beat they wait (up to +5)
  • A shoe about to land on you shows first as a faint outline with a number: how far it will throw you.
  • The ring shows where you'll land. A tick means a crack: you'll bloom there. A cross means bare pavement: the seed is wasted and you stay put.
  • Wrong shoe coming? Duck. It passes over you and you wait for a better one.
  • Bikes ring a bell two beats before they reach you. Duck, or you'll snap back to your previous flower.

Levels, the clock and stars

There are eight streets, from before dawn to night. Each one adds one new idea, and crossing a street unlocks the next.

  • The clock at the top counts down. Reach the curb before it runs out, or "the street went quiet" and you try again.
  • Stars: 3, 2 or 1 for how fast you crossed. Replay a street to beat your stars.

Tips

  • Read the number on the coming shoe, then look for a crack exactly that far away.
  • You don't have to take every shoe. Ducking the wrong one is often the fastest way across.
  • Aim roughly: the ring snaps to a crack in reach if you point near it.
  • At a red light, stay under the waiting crowd: every beat they stand there, you'll fly further.
  • Listen for the bike bell and duck straight away.
  • The more flowers you plant, the fuller the music, and the brighter the street.

If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
The game looks sideways on my phoneTurn the phone to the left. If it then looks upside down, turn it the other way.
No soundTurn the volume up, and check the speaker button (top right) isn't crossed out. On an iPhone, also check the silent switch.
I'm stuck or lost on a streetPause, then restart.
Tapping does nothing, or the screen stays blankReload the page. If an error message appears, take a screenshot.

Playtesting? Tell us what you think

Play for about 10 minutes without asking anyone for help, then send a few lines (a voice note is fine) to the person who shared the game with you:

  1. What did you play on: phone, tablet or computer? Which model and browser?
  2. Did you understand what to do on the first street without help? What confused you?
  3. How far did you get? Which street was the hardest, and did any feel unfair?
  4. Did you find the DUCK button and hear the bike bell? Did ducking make sense?
  5. What was the best moment, and what was the most annoying one?
